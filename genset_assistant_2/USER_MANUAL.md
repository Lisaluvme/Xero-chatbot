# Genset Assistant 2 User Manual

## Introduction

Genset Assistant 2 is a Flutter application designed to assist users in learning about, maintaining, and troubleshooting generators. This app provides educational resources, maintenance schedules, troubleshooting guides, service record tracking, live status monitoring, and contact information for support.

## Installation and Setup

1. **Download the App**: Install the app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store (depending on your device).
2. **Permissions**: Grant necessary permissions for notifications and internet access when prompted.
3. **Initial Setup**: Launch the app. No additional setup is required beyond basic permissions.

## Navigation

The app uses a bottom navigation bar with six main sections:

- **Learn**: Educational content about generators.
- **Maintenance**: Maintenance schedules and tips.
- **Troubleshoot**: Guides for diagnosing and fixing issues.
- **Service**: Track and manage service records.
- **Live Status**: Monitor real-time generator status.
- **Contact**: Get in touch with support.

Tap on any tab to navigate to that section.

## Features

### Learn

This section provides educational materials on generators, including:

- Basic generator components and functions.
- Types of generators.
- Safety guidelines.
- Operational principles.

Browse through articles, videos, or interactive content to build your knowledge.

### Maintenance

Access maintenance schedules and checklists:

- Regular maintenance tasks (e.g., oil changes, filter replacements).
- Seasonal maintenance tips.
- Reminders for upcoming maintenance.
- Step-by-step guides for common maintenance procedures.

Set up notifications for maintenance reminders.

### Troubleshoot

Use this feature to diagnose generator issues:

- Symptom-based troubleshooting guides.
- Common error codes and solutions.
- Diagnostic checklists.
- Step-by-step repair instructions.

Search for specific issues or browse by category (e.g., starting problems, power output issues).

### Service Records

Keep track of all service activities:

- Log new service records with dates, descriptions, and costs.
- View history of past services.
- Set reminders for future services.
- Export or share service records.

### Live Status

Monitor your generator in real-time:

- Current status (running/stopped).
- Fuel levels, voltage, and other parameters.
- Alerts for abnormal conditions.
- Historical data and trends.

Ensure your generator is connected to the app for live monitoring.

### Contact

Get help and support:

- Contact information for technical support.
- Frequently asked questions (FAQ).
- Report bugs or request features.
- Links to user forums or documentation.

## Notifications

The app uses notifications for:

- Maintenance reminders.
- Service due dates.
- Alerts from live status monitoring.

You can customize notification preferences in your device settings.

## Troubleshooting the App

- **App not loading**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection and restart the app.
- **Notifications not working**: Check your device notification settings for the app.
- **Live status not updating**: Verify your generator's connection and app permissions.
- **Data not syncing**: Log out and log back in to refresh data.

If issues persist, contact support through the Contact section.

## Privacy and Security

- The app collects minimal data necessary for functionality.
- All data is encrypted and stored securely.
- No personal information is shared without your consent.

## Updates

Regularly update the app to access new features and bug fixes. Updates are available through your app store.

## Support

For additional help, visit the Contact section in the app or email support@gensetassistant.com.
