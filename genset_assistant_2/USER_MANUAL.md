# Genset Assistant 2 User Manual

## Introduction

Genset Assistant 2 is a Flutter application designed to assist users in learning about, maintaining, and troubleshooting generators. This app provides educational resources, maintenance schedules, troubleshooting guides, service record tracking, live status monitoring, and contact information for support.

## Installation and Setup

1. **Download the App**: Install the app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store (depending on your device).
2. **Permissions**: Grant necessary permissions for notifications and internet access when prompted.
3. **Initial Setup**: Launch the app. No additional setup is required beyond basic permissions.

## Navigation

The app uses a bottom navigation bar with six main sections:

- **Learn**: Educational content about generators.
- **Maintenance**: Maintenance schedules and tips.
- **Troubleshoot**: Guides for diagnosing and fixing issues.
- **Service**: Track and manage service records.

Tap on any tab to navigate to that section.

### How to Operate a Genset

Learn the essential steps for safely operating your MGM generator system:

#### Pre-Start Checklist
- **Fuel Check**: Ensure adequate fuel level and quality.
- **Oil Level**: Verify engine oil is at the proper level.
- **Coolant**: Check radiator coolant level.
- **Battery**: Confirm battery is charged and connections are secure.
- **Emergency Stop**: Test emergency stop button functionality.

#### Starting Your Generator
1. **Preparation**: Ensure all safety precautions are in place.
2. **Control Panel**: Turn the main switch to the "ON" position.
3. **Fuel System**: Open fuel valves if applicable.
4. **Start Sequence**: Press and hold the start button (typically 5-10 seconds).
5. **Warm-up**: Allow the generator to run for 2-3 minutes before connecting loads.

#### Operating Guidelines
- **Load Management**: Never exceed 80% of rated capacity.
- **Monitoring**: Regularly check gauges for oil pressure, coolant temperature, and voltage.
- **Ventilation**: Ensure proper ventilation to prevent carbon monoxide buildup.
- **Noise Levels**: Be aware of noise regulations in your area.

#### Emergency Procedures
- **Overload**: Reduce load immediately if overload occurs.
- **Overheat**: Shut down if temperature exceeds safe limits.
- **Low Oil**: Stop immediately if oil pressure drops.
- **Emergency Stop**: Use emergency stop button for immediate shutdown.

#### Shutdown Procedure
1. **Remove Loads**: Disconnect all electrical loads.
2. **Cool Down**: Allow generator to run at no load for 2-3 minutes.
3. **Fuel System**: Close fuel valves if applicable.
4. **Control Panel**: Turn main switch to "OFF" position.
5. **Battery**: Disconnect if storing for extended periods.

## Troubleshooting the App

- **App not loading**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection and restart the app.
- **Notifications not working**: Check your device notification settings for the app.
- **Live status not updating**: Verify your generator's connection and app permissions.
- **Data not syncing**: Log out and log back in to refresh data.
